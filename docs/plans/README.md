# Historical design plans

These files preserve earlier proposals and milestone notes. They are **not the
current capability, configuration, installation, or production-readiness reference**.
Commands, crate names, completion labels and numerical claims can differ from
the implementation. Do not execute their examples without checking current guides.

- [Project plan](PROJECT_PLAN.md): broader intended platform and future extensions.
- [Kubernetes plan](KUBERNETES_PLAN.md): proposed workload separation/autoscaler design.
- [Work breakdown](WORK_BREAKDOWN.md): historical milestone descriptions, including
  completion labels that do not establish current end-to-end behavior.

Use the [maintained documentation](../README.md),
[configuration reference](../configuration.md), and
[deployment guides](../../deploy/README.md) instead. Source and verified tests
take precedence over historical claims. Old performance numbers are not current
benchmarks without a reproducible workload and measurement procedure.
